# Prompt 8 – Family Meeting Invitation

## Version 1
Write an invitation to a resident’s family for a meeting at the aged-care facility.

## Version 2
Write a polite and professional invitation to a resident’s family for a scheduled meeting. Include the resident’s name, meeting date, time, location, and purpose of the meeting.

## Final Version
You are assisting the administration team in an aged-care facility. Write a short, respectful, and professional invitation to a resident’s family for a scheduled meeting.

Use only the information provided.

Include:
- Resident name
- Family member name, if provided
- Meeting date
- Meeting time
- Meeting location
- Purpose of the meeting
- Any relevant arrival or contact instructions

Keep the message warm, clear, and suitable for professional family communication.

Do not include or assume medical, clinical, or private information that has not been provided.

If important meeting information is missing, clearly state that staff confirmation is required.

## Example Input
Resident name: Susan Clark
Family member name: Emma Clark
Meeting date: 27 August 2026
Meeting time: 1:30 pm
Meeting location: Meeting Room 2
Purpose: Routine care planning discussion
Instructions: Please check in at reception on arrival

## Improvement Notes
Version 1 was too general and did not specify the information needed.

Version 2 added the main meeting details and purpose.

The final version added aged-care context, privacy controls, a clear communication structure, and instructions for handling missing information.
