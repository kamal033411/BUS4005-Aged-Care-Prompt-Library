# Prompt 1 – Meal Preference Update

## Version 1
Create a clear note for staff about a resident’s updated meal preference.

## Version 2
Create a short and professional staff note about a resident’s updated meal preference. Include the resident’s name, previous meal preference, new meal preference, and the date the change starts. Keep the note clear and easy for aged-care staff to understand.

## Final Version
You are assisting the administration team in an aged-care facility. Create a short, professional staff update about a resident’s meal preference change using only the information provided.

Include:
- Resident name
- Previous meal preference
- New meal preference
- Effective date
- Any relevant non-clinical notes provided by staff

Keep the message concise and suitable for internal staff communication.

Do not add or assume allergies, medical conditions, dietary restrictions, or clinical advice that are not provided.

If important information is missing, clearly state that it requires staff confirmation.

## Example Input
Resident name: Mary Wilson  
Previous meal preference: Regular meals  
New meal preference: Vegetarian meals  
Effective date: 12 August 2026  
Notes: Resident prefers softer vegetables.

## Improvement Notes
Version 1 was too general and did not specify what information should be included.

Version 2 added clearer instructions and required resident details.

The final version added a professional role, structured output requirements, and safety controls to reduce the risk of invented medical or dietary information.

Staff Note – Meal Preference Update

Resident: Mary Wilson

## Issues Identified in Version 1

- The prompt was too general and did not define a clear output structure.
- It did not explicitly instruct the AI to use only the information provided.
- It did not include any safety instruction about avoiding assumptions related to allergies, medical conditions, or dietary restrictions.
- It did not explain what to do if important information was missing.
